Korupsi · 10/11Lirik di BandcampEnglish: unofficial translation

Korupsi adalah album perdana Ledakan Pemuda. Rekaman pertama dibuat di Jakarta pada 2019, di tengah gelombang unjuk rasa. Produser Patrick Padgett merampungkannya dari Amerika Serikat selama enam tahun berikutnya dan merilisnya pada 1 Juni 2025.

Alat musik tradisional Indonesia dibenturkan dengan bass dubstep modern. Vokal Adi Saputra dan Mukti Satria, direkam pada sesi 2019, diimbangi oleh Vani.

Liriknya bicara tentang kecanduan, kematian, amarah, dan perlawanan: satu lagu tentang unjuk rasa 2019, yang lain tentang botol dan pil, bayang-bayang di kamar gelap, roh leluhur, dan pocong di bawah pohon kamboja. Sebelas lagu, 36 menit, dibuka dan ditutup dengan instrumental.

Korupsi is the debut album by Ledakan Pemuda. The first recordings were made in Jakarta in 2019, during the protests. Producer Patrick Padgett finished the record from the United States over the next six years and released it on June 1, 2025. Traditional Indonesian instruments against modern dubstep bass. The vocals of Adi Saputra and Mukti Satria, from the 2019 sessions, are counterbalanced by Vani. The lyrics are about addiction, mortality, anger and revolution: one song about the 2019 protests, the others about the bottle and the pill, shadows in a dark room, ancestral spirits, and a pocong under the frangipani tree. Eleven tracks, 36 minutes, framed by an instrumental intro and outro.
